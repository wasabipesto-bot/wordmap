    About wordmap

    Each dot is one of the 50,000 most common English words. Words are placed so that words a few letters apart sit near each other. The distance is Levenshtein distance: how many single-letter insertions, deletions or substitutions turn one word into the other (cat → cot is 1, cat → coast is 2). A 2-D map can't keep every one of those distances. It is built with UMAP from each word's 15 nearest words, so small neighbourhoods are faithful and long distances across the map are not. Click a word to see its true neighbours.

    What you'll see is mostly length (short words at one end, long words at the other) and shared endings: the -ing, -tion and -ness families form their own regions. densMAP also keeps how crowded each neighbourhood is. Base forms drops inflections (runs, ran, running) to show structure beyond grammar endings.
